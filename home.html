Strategic plan 2026 to 2029, objective 2
2. Food within reach of every Northside family
By 2029, no family in Northside lives more than a 15-minute walk from a free pantry day, and families shape what the pantry stocks.
Goal 2.1: open a second pantry day at the Northside community centre by April.
Goal 2.2: ask families what they need every month, and bring the answers to the board each quarter.
Goal 2.3: train twelve volunteer drivers so homebound neighbours get a delivery each week.
Goal 2.4: report meals served on the public dashboard, updated on the first Monday of each month.
Partners on this objective are drawn from the ecosystem map: the school board, the city and two farms.
